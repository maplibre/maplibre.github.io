Results are in for the 2026 MapLibre Governing Board election, and we're excited to share them alongside a warm welcome to our newest Voting Members. A big thanks to everyone who were part of the election process this year!

- [Stefan Karschti](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre/discussions/507#discussioncomment-18012804) (incumbent)

## 📊 Key Election Statistics

- Total eligible voters (Voting Members): 172
- Ballots cast: 103
- Voter turnout: ~60%
- Seats available: 5 (out of 7 candidates)
- Election method: Conducted online using [OpaVote](https://opavote.com/), with ranked-choice voting (Meek STV).

👉 Checkout the detailed election results on [GitHub](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre/tree/main/2026-polls).

## 🗳️ Growing our pool of Voting Members

Ahead of this year's board election, we ran a [voting members nomination round](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre/issues/506). Voting Members are the ones who elect the Governing Board and vote on any changes to the MapLibre Charter.

That process brought in 31 new Voting Members this August, and we're glad to have them on board as MapLibre keeps growing. The complete [list of voting members, now 172 members strong](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre/blob/main/VOTING_MEMBERS.md), is available in our GitHub documentation.

Thank you again to every candidate, Voting Member, and community member who made this year's election possible.
