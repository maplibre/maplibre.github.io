The [MapLibre Board](https://maplibre.org/news/2026-09-10-board-elections-2026/) wishes to thank all the community members who have offered their valuable feedback regarding [the onboarding of Navara into the MapLibre organization](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre/issues/509). We recognize that there have been concerns regarding both the onboarding process and the long-term strategy for MapLibre as a project and community. We want to stress that board actions are not always perfect, and we are committed to revisiting our approach and supporting the community going forward. To this end, we support strengthening the onboarding process by [requiring consensus](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre/pull/550) and community engagement.

We would like to assure the community that the onboarding of Navara does not mean our funds shift away from our core rendering libraries MapLibre GL JS and MapLibre Native. From our perspective, Navara represents an opportunity to experiment with ​​next-generation map rendering as well as photorealistic 3D rendering in our community, which aligns with [MapLibre strategy](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1AIwWuNw4BbN0qCUIw_WDk9VTMwChzmgg/view) published in 2023. At the same time, we believe that this affiliation will pull Navara into the MapLibre ecosystem, promoting gradual improvements to interoperability with the rest of our ecosystem for those who do choose Navara for their use case.

We understand that this is a topic with strong feelings all around. We want to channel this community’s sentiment toward the best possible future for all the projects involved, particularly the ones that have gotten MapLibre to where we are today.
